Project Identification & Challenge Definition

Protecting UAE's 1.2 Million Outdoor Workers Under Extreme 48°C+ Climate Strain

🎯 The Problem Mandate

Over 1.2 million outdoor industrial, construction, and logistics workers in Abu Dhabi face summer temperatures exceeding 48°C with lethal humidity. Heat exhaustion deteriorates rapidly into heat stroke without early warning.

Current industrial solutions fail: importing $820+ wearable bands per worker creates prohibitive capex, severe dust degradation, charging logistics headaches, and English-only warnings that migrant workforces cannot comprehend.

⚡ The HeatGuard Solution

HeatGuard eliminates proprietary wearable capex entirely by assimilating ambient microclimate telemetry with physiological thermodynamics (ISO 7933 PHS) on workers' existing smartphones.

  • Proactive Warning: Detects rising strain 15–30 minutes before symptoms manifest.
  • 5 Dialects Spoken: Spoken audio alerts in Urdu, Hindi, Tagalog, Bengali, Arabic.
  • Dignity & Privacy: Zero employer surveillance, tamper-proof OSHAD audit trails.

📐 ISO 7933 Predicted Heat Strain (PHS)

Heat balance equation: S = M - W - (R + C + E + RES)

  • M: Metabolic rate determined by worker's trade (250–450 W/m²).
  • R + C: Radiative & convective heat exchange with ambient desert air.
  • E_req vs E_max: Evaporative sweat requirement vs maximum evaporative capacity dictated by relative humidity.
  • Core Temp (Tcore): Clinically capped at 38.5°C limit before heat exhaustion.

🎯 Personalization Without Invasive Surveillance

HeatGuard dynamically adjusts physiological risk thresholds based on consented, non-clinical operational variables:

  • Acclimatization State: Unacclimatized workers (Days 1–5 in UAE) trigger early alerts at 30°C WBGT.
  • PPE Insulation Factor (clo): Heavy coveralls vs vented high-vis gear.
  • Continuous Work Duration: Tracks uninterrupted shift time dynamically.
  • 15–30 Minute Predictive Lead Time: Intervenes before physical collapse.
