### The Unfair Advantage:
- **Zero Capex:** Equipping 1,000 workers with SlateSafety costs **$820,000+** in hardware upfront. HeatGuard costs **$0** in hardware capex, running on ambient site IoT telemetry and workers' existing Android smartphones.

## 4. 🐟 MiroFish 100-Agent Swarm Simulation (`mirofish-simulation.md`)

Simulating 100 agents across 5 distinct ecosystem swarms yielded high-signal strategic insights:

1. **HSE Directors (Swarm Temp: 🟢 Supportive | 6.5/10):**  
   Ready to buy if onboarding is turnkey and pilot shows zero site disruption. High interest in meeting Abu Dhabi OSHAD and MOHRE safety compliance.
2. **Migrant Workers (Swarm Temp: 🟡 Skeptical | 5.5/10):**  
   Identified as the key operational risk. Workers feared employers would use health flags to deduct pay or terminate contracts. **Actionable Fix:** Implement strict client-side encryption and worker-anonymized aggregate dashboards. Employers only see "Crew Status: 2 workers resting", never individual personal medical identifiers.
3. **Abu Dhabi Health Authority / Regulators (Swarm Temp: 🟢 Supportive | 7.5/10):**  
   Strong enthusiasm for population-level heat intelligence. Requires clear disclaimers distinguishing screening from diagnosis and alignment with local health data sovereignty laws.
4. **Hackathon Judges (Swarm Temp: 💚 Enthusiastic | 8.2/10):**  
   Judges favored the project because it is deeply contextualized to the UAE, utilizes Microsoft Azure's native IoT and AI stack, and addresses a life-or-death scenario with an interactive multilingual demo.
